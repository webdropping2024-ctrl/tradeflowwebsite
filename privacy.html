Tradeflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Tradeflow is a desktop trading journal application designed to help users record and analyze their trading activity.

2. Information We Collect

Tradeflow may use Google OAuth to allow users to authenticate with their Google account.

When you sign in with Google, Tradeflow may receive basic account information such as your name, email address and profile information, depending on the permissions granted during authentication.

3. How We Use Information

Information obtained through Google authentication is used to provide account authentication and access to the features of Tradeflow.

4. Data Storage

Tradeflow only stores information that is necessary to provide its features. Users' trading journal information is handled according to the application's functionality and configuration.

5. Google User Data

Tradeflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

6. Data Sharing

Tradeflow does not sell users' personal information.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the application.

8. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through the official Tradeflow contact information.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
